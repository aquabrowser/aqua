Aqua Browser - portable data

This folder holds your Aqua profile: the encrypted vault (aqua.db) with your
tabs, history, cookies, site data, bookmarks and settings, plus filter list
updates. Because it sits next to Aqua Browser.exe, Aqua keeps everything here
instead of on the computer it runs on.

- Move or copy the whole "Aqua Browser" folder to take your browser with you.
- Without your master password the vault can't be read.
- Delete this folder's contents to start over with a new profile.
